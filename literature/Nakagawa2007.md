![figures/Nakagawa2007/fig-10-x94-y633.png](figures/Nakagawa2007/fig-10-x94-y633.png)


*Anatomic location of the compact AV node and the rightward and leftward inferior extensions of the AV node in a human heart. The RIE has the longest or slowest conduction time (anisotropy) and participates in most forms of AVNRT. The earliest retrograde activation of the RIE is between teh tricuspid annulus and CS ostia. The LIE is quicker, with earliest activation recorded about 2 cm from CS ostia on the roof.*
