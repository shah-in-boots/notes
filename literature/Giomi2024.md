> As a first step to localize GPs areas, a fragmentation bipolar map was created, using a threshold of 2 deflections at a filtering setting of 200500 Hz, as proposed by Aksu and colleagues.4 A second frequency map was then created using the OTNF by adjusting the frequency window to maximize the isolation of highfrequency areas. Peak frequency was set at 600 Hz to highlight the smallest areas with high-frequency spectrum in the  anatomic GP positions  


*Approach to identification using bandpass filtering and peak frequency emphasis, overlaying maps to identify reasonable areas for GP to be ablated.*

C: Ablation tags on (from top to bottom): 1 5 superior paraseptal ganglionated plexi (SPSGP); 2 5 PRAGP; 3 5 inferior paraseptal ganglionated plexi (IPSGP); 4 5 SLAGP. White boxes: representative electrograms of high-frequency area, high-fragmentation area, and normal myocardium. IVC 5 inferior vena cava; LIPV 5 left inferior pulmonary vein; LSPV 5 left superior pulmonary vein; SVC 5 superior vena cava.*
