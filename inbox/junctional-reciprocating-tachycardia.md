Although described as a tachycardia diagnosis, is actually an accessory pathway, as outlined in [accessory-pathway-variants](permanent/accessory-pathway-variants.md). 

- Accessory pathway with retrograde-only conduction
- Decremental properties, with long retrograde conduction time
- It can lead to incessant long-RP tachycardia.
- Commonly posteroseptal
