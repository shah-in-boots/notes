These maneuvers are described in @Veenhuyzen2011 and @Veenhuyzen2012, which are detailed in [Veenhuyzen2011](literature/Veenhuyzen2011.md) and [Veenhuyzen2012](literature/Veenhuyzen2012.md)

# His-refractory PVCs

- PVC given during His being refractory
- Can be up to 35-55 ms earlier than anticipated His potential to be considered refractory
- AVNRT and AT can be modified by His-synchronous PVC
- His-refractory PVC in AVNRT with accessory pathway can advance the next A through AP, but will not advance the next V (because AP is bystander)
- if AVRT, would advance and reset the tachycardia
- if AVRT, could also delay next A
- if AVRT, could terminate tachycardia with VA block
- PVC that terminates arrhythmia without getting to atria rules out AT

# Early VOP responses

- fusion can be seen if VOP is 10-30 ms faster than TCL
- if fused beats advance the tachycardia than AP is present
- if fused beats advance tachycardia wiht same atrial activation sequence, than AP is part of circuit
- if fusion repeatedly terminates the tachycardia, then AP present and part of tachycardia and participating
- non-fused beats that terminate (e.g. fully paced), then rules out AT but AVNRT still posssible
