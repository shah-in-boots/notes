During ablation, one thing to be careful of is monitoring AV conduction on septal pathways. 
As seen below, what appears to be loss of preexcitation is actually a junctional escape, suggesting proximity to the AV node. 
RF ablation should be terminated immediately.

![EGMs seen during ablation of posteroseptal accessory pathway. Subsequently followed by junctional escape, masking the pre-excitation.](resources/ablation-wpw.png)
