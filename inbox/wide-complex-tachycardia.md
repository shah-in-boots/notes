obviously authored by a great in the field, [Hein-Wellens-MD](permanent/Hein-Wellens-MD.md).

The differential for a wide complex tachycardia are the following...

1. Supraventricular tachycardia
	- Pre-existing or functional bundle branch block
	- Bizzare baseline QRS (e.g. ACHD, myopathy)
	- Drug excess or electrolyte abnormalities
	- Anterograde conduction via accessory pathway (i.e. WPW)
1. Ventricular tachycardia
1. Ventricular pacing
1. Artifact (e.g. rhythm strip)
