@Swerdlow2020 reviews the issues with measuring impedance to assess the condition of leads.

More importantly, when leads fracture, they can show different properties at difference frequencies, which is useful in assessing underlying etiology. 

When leads fracture, the most common or first sign of things to be abnormal are:

1. Impedance changes or oversensing
1. Oversensing with short intervals (fast rates)
1. Patient-specific impedance trend changes
1. Impedance $\ge$ 2500
