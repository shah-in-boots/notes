VA linking describes the relationship of the last entrained ventricular signal after [atrial-overdrive-pacing](permanent/atrial-overdrive-pacing.md) or [diastolic-PACs](inbox/diastolic-PACs.md) to the subsequent, earliest atrial signal.

*An alternative to VA linking that is similar is HA linking, using the His EGM as a source of truth.*

Described in [Maruyama2007](literature/Maruyama2007.md), and suggests that differential AOP can be used to identify a $\Delta\ VA$ time from multiple sites, which if very similar to each other can rule out AT.
