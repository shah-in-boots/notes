Guiding principles...

1. Define the abnormal anatomy, using both bipolar and unipolar signal, with goal to identify regions that are endocardial, myocardial, and epicardial. Chamber details in [normal-chamber-voltage](inbox/normal-chamber-voltage.md)
1. Analyze ECG of VT and recordings in regions of abnormal signal (both sinus and pacing)
1.  Surrogates for VT exit sites include...
	- 12-lead morphology of VT 
	- pace-mapping with short $S \textendash QRS$ that matches clinical VT QRS
1. Surrogate for isthmuses of the VT circuit include...
	- pace-mapping with long $S \textendash QRS$ time
	- regions with dramatic changes to QRS morphology with pacing
	- late potentials in sinus or pacing
	- Multicomponent or complex EGMs brought out with pacing (LAVA)
	- Channels based on voltage, late potentials, inexcitability
1. Evaluate tissue characteristics on EAM, looking for areas of isochronal crowding and abnormal signal
