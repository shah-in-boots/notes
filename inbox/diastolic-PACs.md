Late-coupled PACs, which are essentially His-refractory or His-synchronous (or His-committed) PACs.
Conceptually similar to [atrial-overdrive-pacing](permanent/atrial-overdrive-pacing.md) and are a type of brief entrainment, [entrainment-maneuvers](permanent/entrainment-maneuvers.md) touches on the topic.

# Responses

[atrioventricular-nodal-reentrant-tachycardia](inbox/atrioventricular-nodal-reentrant-tachycardia.md)

During AVNRT, a late PAC can enter/exit the AVN through one of many atrionodal pathways, but can only get to the ventricle through the His-Purkinje system, whether or not there is a lower common pathway. 
Late-coupled PACs can either reset or terminate AVNRT (depending on how the wavefronts collide in the AVN circuits, descrbed in detail in [anatomy-atrioventricular-node](permanent/anatomy-atrioventricular-node.md).
When termination occurs, rules out JT.

[junctional-tachycardia](inbox/junctional-tachycardia.md)

A late PAC can only fuse with the atrial activation from JT, thus cannot reset or terminate the rhythm.
The exception is if a *bystander pathway* is present.
