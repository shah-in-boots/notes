# Left ventricle


The normal bipolar voltage has been considered $> 1.62\ mV$ however generally accepted to use $>1.5\ mV$ as normal (as it averages with the RV values). 
Unipolar voltage was considered to be normal at $>8.3\ mV$ in a study looking at endocardial and epicardial bipolar voltages, and comparing them to unipolar findings. 
Caveat is this is only in patients with normal endocardial bipolar voltage.
Data is from @Hutchinson2011.


Dense scar is considered $< 0.5\ mV$

# Right ventricle

Normal bipolar voltage is considered $> 1.47\ mV$, which end up be considered averaged to $>1.5\ mV$, similar to LV.
Unipolar values for normal are $> 5.5\ mV$, as described (as above) in @Hutchinson2011
