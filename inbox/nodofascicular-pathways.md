Nodofascicular pathways historically were thought to be Mahaim fibers due to its nodal/decremental properties, however they are not atriofascicular pathways. Can see [atriofascicular-accessory-pathways](permanent/atriofascicular-accessory-pathways.md) as reference.
Are part of the family of variant accessory pathways, outlined in [accessory-pathway-variants](permanent/accessory-pathway-variants.md).

Can be part of tachycardia as both anterograde and retrograde limb as either narrow complex tachycardia or pre-excited tachycardia.
