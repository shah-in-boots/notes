## Voltage clamps

For $I$, currents that are downward represent cationic current flowing into the cell (e.g. depolarization).
For example, the current can wane despite a voltage that is being applied consistently $\rightarrow$ *inactivating current*
Here, in the first example below, , is a sodium channel, but similar to calcium channels as well.
The other example shown is a potassium current, and notably has a slow, delayed activation and transfer cationic current outwards $\rightarrow$ *delayed rectifier, similar to other $K+$ currents*
