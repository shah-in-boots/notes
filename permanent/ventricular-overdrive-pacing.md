Ventricular overdrive pacing (**VOP**) during SVT can have several responses.
Can review in light of [entrainment-maneuvers](permanent/entrainment-maneuvers.md) and [atrial-overdrive-pacing](permanent/atrial-overdrive-pacing.md) maneuvers.
When retrograde conduction is present...

- $VAV$ response $\rightarrow$ oAVRT or AVNRT
- $VAAV$ response $\rightarrow$ AT, although rarely can be aAVNRT
- Pseudo $VAV$ response when no retrograde conduction $\rightarrow$ AT
- Pseudo $VAAV$ $\rightarrow$ aAVNRT or oAVRT, or slowly conducting AT
- $PPI - TCL > 115\ ms$ or $SA - VA > 85\ ms$ $\rightarrow$ AVNRT (otherwise it is oAVRT)
- If the atria accelerate to PCL in <2 fully paced QRS or in the presence of ventricular fusion $\rightarrow$ oAVRT otherwise is AVNRT

In situations where retrograde conduction is known to be absent...

- AT is almost always the answer
- Cannot rule out nodoventricular (oNVRT) or nodofascicular (oNFRT) reentry
- AVNRT with lower common pathways block (His absent) or upper common pathway block (His present)
