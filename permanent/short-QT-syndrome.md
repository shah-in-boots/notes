The opposite of long QT syndromes essentially. 
Its defined as $QT < 360\ ms$, and with correction is defined as $QTc < 320\ ms$.
Generally speak they have currents that are doing the opposite of long QT, with increased $K+$ current or decreased $Ca^{++}$
