Automaticity of SA node is based off of two *clocks*

- membrane voltage clock: balance between outward $I_K$ currents and inward $I_{Kf}$ currents
- calcium clock: rhythmic release of $Ca^{++}$ from sarcoplasmic reticulum, which when reaches $\uparrow$ $[Ca^{++}]$ will activate *NCX* channels leading to depolarization

Pathology of SA node can be broken down to intrinsic vs. extrinsic factors
- ablation of cristal AT, SVC isolation, LA roof damaging SAN artery can lead to SA damage
