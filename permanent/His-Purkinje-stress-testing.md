Methods to test His-Purkinje system

1. Burst pacing = when $PCL < 450\ ms$ leads to block or prolongation of HV interval
1. Procainamide challenge = doubling of HV interval or extending $HV > 100\ ms$, or seeing intra/infra-His block
1. Atropine/isoproterenol challenge = AV node will block prior to the HPS, thus protecting HPS... improving AV conduction may unmask HPS disease
