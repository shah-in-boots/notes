Essentially this is a an accessory AV node that failed to involute. 
Usually has both a nodal component, and a His-like component. 
Briefly...

- Described also has __Mahaim fibers__.
- They are anterograde-only conduction with decremental properties and are adenosine-responsive.
- Participate in antidromic AVRT, with RBB as anterograde limb, and His/AVN as retrograde limb
- Behaves like an accessory AV node or His-like structure, and exists along the posterolateral tricuspid annulus (**TA**), inserting into distal RBB (moderator band 85% of the time) or adjacent RV

*There is a variant that can exist where the insertion is directly into the RV myocardium, which will change the speed of His activation (as retrograde limb)*

Extensive work detailing this pattern done by [Sonny-Jackman-MD](permanent/Sonny-Jackman-MD.md), seen in this detailed study -  [McClelland1994](literature/McClelland1994.md).
