The hallmark original paper by Kistler was in 2001, and has been updated in 2021 by @Kistler2021 . 

![P wave localization algorithm for focal ATs](figures/Kistler2021/fig-3-x281-y508.png)

The algorithmic approach to localize P waves remains as based on vectorcardiography approaches, however emphasizes the value of...
- *V1* as the initial lead to identify chamber
- Inferior leads to identify superiority
- *V2* to *V4* provide the anterior/posterior descriminator as well for RA

![Example of P wave morphology in focal ATs from different regions](figures/Kistler2021/fig-6-x119-y77.png)
