# Confirmation of block

As referenced in the [lines-of-block](permanent/lines-of-block.md) note, typical flutter has a very well-studied approach to assessing block.

## Crista shunt

A mimic of failed isthmus block is through __crista shunt__, where although the CTI is blocked, there is an area of conduction available through the crista terminalis that allow for it to look like there is leak along the line.
The following schematic, seen in [Anselme2001](literature/Anselme2001.md), gives EGM-guided understanding of what a crista shunt may look like.
