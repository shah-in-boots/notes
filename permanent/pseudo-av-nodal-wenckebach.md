Example of traditional AV nodal Wenckebach physiology with increasingly rapid A1 train.

![AV nodal Wenckebach](resources/av-nodal-wenckebach.png)

However have to ensure capture of the atria to claim AV nodal Wenckebach.
Can occur for multiple reasons...

- failure to capture the atria due to the catheter
- atrial refractory period reached
- collision of other atrial stimuli causing pacing on refractory tissue

![Shows pseudo-AV nodal Wenckebach with failure of capture of the atria, as seen on His catheter)](resources/av-nodal-wenckebach-1.png)

![Atrial stimuli fails to capture, but this is due to AV nodal atrial echo, as the previous conducted beat then goes back up the fast pathway, depolarizing the atria prematurely](resources/pseudo-av-nodal-wenckebach.png)
