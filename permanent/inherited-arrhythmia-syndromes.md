1. Electrical syndromes = LQTS, BrS, SQTS, CPVT, ERS, etc
1. Mechanical syndromes = ACM, HCM, DCM, etc

# Early repolarization syndrome (ERS)

Patients with SCD or aborted idiopathic VF will occasionally have an underlying channelopathy.
ECG changes are J-point elevations in inferior leads or lateral leads >0.1 mV
