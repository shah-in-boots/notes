@Ly2025 looked at QRS fragmentation in cardiomyopathy patients and finds patterns that appear to predict ICD therapy risk. QRS amplitude < 31 uV in V1, QRS peak width of 4-8 ms in V1, and final 25% of QRS location in V1 seemed to be most predictive (better than clinical factors). 1/5 of ~100 patient sample with ICD firing in 2-4 year range.

Methods are interesting - take a global QRS that is smoothed to identify normal peaks, and compare that to peaks seen on individual QRS templates (RV pacing).
