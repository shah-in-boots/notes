__Ventriculophasic sinus arrhythmia__ or the __ventriculophasic response__ 

Context: when there is high degree of AV block such that SA node is affected by hemodynamics, mechanical forces, and ANS system leading to changes in SA firing rate

Definition: $P \textemdash P$ surrounding QRS is shorter than $P \textemdash P$ without QRS complex

Mechanism: Likely multifactorial

1. Due to increase RA pressure from ventricular squeeze, leads to electromechanical feedback causing increased SA firing rate
1. Increased SAN artery systolic pressure due to ventricular systole leads to increased SA perfusion, increasing firing rate [@Hashimoto1967]
