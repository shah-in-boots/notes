Differential diagnosis of NCT based on RP intervals.

| Probability | None | Short | Medium | Long |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **High** | tAVNRT | oAVRT | AT | AT |
| **Mid** | JT | aAVNRT | aAVNRT | aAVNRT |
| **Low** | AT ($PR=AH$) | AT | oAVRT | oAVRT |

Limited however interpretation when intracardiac EGMs are available, as seen below. 
The SVT could be classified as *short*, *mid*, or *long* RP interval based on where it is measured from.
