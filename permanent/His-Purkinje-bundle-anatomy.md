His bundle is approximately 1 cm in length, well-insulated (thus measurable His potentials preceding ventricular potentials on EGM)

Divides into the thinner right bundle branch (**RBB**) and left bundle branch (**LBB**)

RBB is a discrete superficial fiber, both proximal and distal, dives into muscle at mid septum, and exits onto moderator band

LBB is a wider structure that branches early into an anterior fascicle (**LAF**), which is thinner, a left posterior fascicle (**LPF**), which is broader, and sometimes there exists a left septal fascicle (**LSF**)

- LAF is thinner and more prone to injury from ischemia/infarct
- LSF blocks more frequently with age
- LPF more resistant to damage

The frequency of bundle branch blocks as below...

$$
LAFB > RBBB > LBB > LPFB
$$

Can assess disease in conduction system as well [His-Purkinje-stress-testing](permanent/His-Purkinje-stress-testing.md)
