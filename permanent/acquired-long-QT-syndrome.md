 The channelopathies version of LQTS is in [long-QT-syndrome](permanent/long-QT-syndrome.md).
 An acquired type can also occur and is multifactorial.
 Predominant issue is binding to *HERG* channel, leading to decrease in $I_{Kr}$.

 Summary of causes:

 - Drugs
 - Acute or chronic ischemic heart disease
 - Electrolyte abnormalities 
 - Intracranial trauma, hemorrhage, infarction

 The QT is normal at baseline and with exercise and exercise recovery.
 May have polygenic variability, with decreased repolarization reserve
