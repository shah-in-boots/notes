Functional reentry has multiple subtypes, and is part of the core arrhythmia mechanisms, as seen in [arrhythmia-mechanisms](permanent/arrhythmia-mechanisms.md)

- leading circle reentry
- spiral wave reentry
- anisotropic reentry

# Spiral wave reentry

Properties:

- the circuit is very small, with the $\lambda \approx PL$
- continuously has an excitable center (non-refractory)
- collision and wave breaks can lead to fibrillatory conduction patterns with the arms of the spiral
- can rotate and shift or stabilize into specific pathways
- transient, small excitable gap is present
