The three major arrhythmia mechanisms...

- Enhanced automaticity (normal vs. abnormal)
- Triggered afterdepolarizations (early vs. delayed)
- Reentry (anatomical and functional)

Goes into further detail in @Antzelevitch2011 from basic science perspective.
Mechanisms are not exclusive. 
Enhanced automaticity and triggered afterdepolarizations are issues with impulse initiation, while re-entry is an issue with impulse propagation. 

# Enhanced automaticity

__Enhanced normal__ automaticity is when normal tissue increases in automaticity, like the sinus or AV node. 
Abnormal would wehn secondary tissue types speed up past normal pacemakers, which is why considerd abnormal.
Findings that are suggestive...
- warm-up pattern highly suggestive of automatic focus
- transient suppression with over-drive then re-warmup
- rate may increase with sympathetic stimulation e.g. ischemia, stretch, hypokalemia ($\uparrow$ phase IV depolarization)

# Trigger afterdepolarizations

Overall considered teh stimulation of a myocyte under non-physiologic conditions give rise to pathologic, non-driven upstrokes.
Classified as early after depolarizations (__EAD__) and delayed after depolarizations (__DAD__).

![Table differentiating between EAD and DADs](resources/arrhythmia-mechanisms.png)

Primarily, EADs are worsened with longer QTs, while DADs are instigated by excess intracellular calcium, usually from sarcoplasmic reticulum.

# Reentry

Requirements for reentry...

- two pathways
- heterogenous electrical properties
- block in one, while propagates over other

An __excitable gap__ must exist based on those requirements (the gap between the wavefront and the already-depolarized tissue), otherwise the *dog would catch its tail*.
Stimulation at any point in the excitable gap will allow for a propagating impulse that will __reset__ the tachycardia or circuit.

$$
\lambda = CV \times RP
$$

The wavelength is a product of hte conduction velocity and refractory period of the arrhythmia and the tissue that is sustaining it.
If the $\lambda > path\ length$, then the arrhythmia would terminate.

Real circuits, like AVNRT, AVRT, scar-mediated VT are heterogenous in each of these properties and the duration of the excitable gap can also wobble.

Functional re-entry is more complicated, described in [functional-reentry](permanent/functional-reentry.md)
