simplified __Nernst equation__ for cardiac action potentials

---

---

$$
E_{I} = \frac{-61.5}{z} \times log_{10}(\frac{[I]_{o}}{[I]_{i}})
$$

Where $z = valence$ of the ion and the constant $\frac{RT}{F} = -61.5$
