__Mahaim fibers__

---

---

Atriofascicular accessory pathways with decremental properties (include adenosine sensitivity).
Participate in aAVRT, with anterograde limb going from posterolateral tricuspid annulus to moderator band (RBB) predominantly (*there exists a variant with insertion into adjacent RV myocardium directly*)
