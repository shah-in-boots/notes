Mechanism of arrhythmia in CPVT?

---

Delayed after-depolarizations


$Ca^{++}$ normally is released from sarcoplasmic reticilum (SR) through the *RYR2* channels.
In CPVT, the *RYR2* channels (or calsequestrin) are leaky, leading to increased cytosolic $Ca^{++}$, which is then removed through NCX ($\downarrow Na^+ \times 3 = \uparrow Ca^{++} \times 1$), which in turn leads to delayed after-depolarizations (DADs) that trigger AP and arrhythmias.
