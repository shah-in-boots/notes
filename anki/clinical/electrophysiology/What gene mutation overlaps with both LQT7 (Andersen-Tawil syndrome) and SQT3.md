What gene mutation overlaps with both __LQT7__ (Andersen-Tawil syndrome) and __SQT3__?

---

__KCNJ2__, responsible for the $I_{K1}$ inward rectifier current

In LQT7, there is a $LOF$ in KCNJ2, while in SQT3 there is a $GOF$ in KCNJ2, leading to the opposite effects
