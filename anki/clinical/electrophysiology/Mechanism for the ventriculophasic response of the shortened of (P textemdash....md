Mechanism for the ventriculophasic response of the shortened of $P \textemdash P$  surrounding a QRS?

---

1. Due to increase RA pressure from ventricular squeeze, leads to electromechanical feedback causing increased SA firing rate
1. Increased SAN artery systolic pressure due to ventricular systole leads to increased SA perfusion, increasing firing rate
