Is this an appropriate ablation location? Why?

![](../../../resources/unipolar-and-bipolar-electrograms-6.png)

---

It meets the three criteria proposed by Jackman. 

1. Ablation catheter is at the site of earliest far-field activation along the crista
1. Unipolar and bipolar near-field signal
1. `Qs` pattern seen on distal unipolar signal

Ablation in this location led to increased automaticity and subsequent cessation of arrhythmia
