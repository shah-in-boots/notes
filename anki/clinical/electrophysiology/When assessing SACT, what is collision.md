When assessing SACT, what is __collision__?

---

Collision is where the A2 (premature) beat does not change the overall cycle length, as it collides with a refractory SA node (and thus does not lead to SA slowing).

$$
A1 \textendash A1 \times 2 = A2 \textendash A3
$$

where...

$$
\begin{align}
A = A1 &= \text{spontaneous atrial beat} \\
A_T = A2 &= \text{atrial premature beat} \\
A_R = A3 &= \text{atrial return beat}
\end{align}
$$
