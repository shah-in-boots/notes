__Atypical fast-slow AVNRT with clockwise reentry__

The anterograde limb is the left inferior extension, while the retrograde limb is the right inferior extension.
The fast pathway is activated in an antegrade/orthodromic fashion, through the lower common pathway, and subsequently activates His bundle.

*Technically, the fast pathway is not part of the cicuit.*
